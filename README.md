# 🥭 Why MANGOS?

**MANGOS** is inspired by the companies shaping the future of Artificial Intelligence and modern computing:

- **M** — **Meta**
- **A** — **Anthropic**
- **N** — **NVIDIA**
- **G** — **Google**
- **O** — **OpenAI**
- **S** — **SpaceX**

These organizations are setting the benchmark for engineering excellence across AI, distributed systems, cloud computing, accelerated computing, and large-scale software platforms.

The vision behind **MANGOS** is to provide a structured learning platform that equips engineers with the knowledge, problem-solving mindset, and practical understanding needed to prepare for careers at world-class technology companies.

Whether your goal is to join **Meta, Anthropic, NVIDIA, Google, OpenAI, SpaceX**, or any other leading technology organization, MANGOS is designed to strengthen your fundamentals, deepen your technical expertise, and help you become interview-ready for high-impact engineering roles.

> **Note:** *MANGOS is an independent educational initiative. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta, Anthropic, NVIDIA, Google, OpenAI, or SpaceX.*

# 📖 About

MANGOS is a centralized knowledge repository created to consolidate practical learning material for Data Engineering and AI into one place. Instead of searching across blogs, videos, documentation, and scattered notes, this repository provides structured learning resources that can be used for interview preparation, day-to-day reference, and continuous upskilling.

# 💡 Why I Started This Repository

Throughout my career, I created personal notes while learning new technologies, solving production challenges, preparing for certifications, and mentoring engineers. Over time, these notes evolved into comprehensive handbooks.

I strongly believe that **mastering the fundamentals is the key to becoming an exceptional engineer**. Technologies change rapidly, but strong fundamentals remain valuable throughout an engineering career.

This repository brings those learnings together in one place so engineers can spend less time searching for information and more time building great solutions.
