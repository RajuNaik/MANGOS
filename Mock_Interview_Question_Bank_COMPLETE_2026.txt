1. HOW TO USE THIS BANK
================================================================================

  - Difficulty tags: [E] Easy/screening-level, [M] Mid-level onsite, [H]
    Hard/senior-level or bar-raiser-style.
  - Company flavor tags: [AMZN] [GOOG] [META] [NFLX] [DBX] [SNOW] [GEN]
    (generic/any company) — reflects the STYLE of question that
    company's loops disproportionately favor, not that the question is
    exclusive to them.
  - Pointers like "-> SD.P3" mean System Design handbook, Problem 3;
    "-> SI.6" means Spark Internals handbook, Section 6. Use these to
    go build/verify your answer against the source material, not to
    skip the thinking.
  - Best practice: answer OUT LOUD or in writing before checking the
    pointer. If you go straight to the handbook, you're studying, not
    drilling — do both, at different times.
  - Recommended cadence: once you've been through Phases 1-6 of the
    Master Index study sequence, spend the final 1-2 weeks doing FULL
    mock loops (Section 12) rather than isolated question drilling.

================================================================================
2. ROUND TYPES YOU'LL ACTUALLY FACE
================================================================================

  PHONE SCREEN / OA: 1-2 SQL or coding problems, sometimes a lightweight
    "tell me about a project" — Tracks A/G, [E]-[M] difficulty.
  TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE: "walk me through a project you built" followed by
    increasingly pointed follow-ups probing whether you actually
    understand what you built — Tracks A-C, F, H, all difficulties.
  SYSTEM DESIGN: one open-ended design prompt, 45 minutes — Track D.
  CODING: 1-2 algorithmic problems, sometimes SQL-as-algorithm — Track G.
  BAR RAISER / HIRING MANAGER: mix of technical + behavioral, often the
    most senior interviewer, asks the hardest follow-ups across every
    track — treat every track's [H] questions as bar-raiser material.
  BEHAVIORAL: not deeply covered in this file (a dedicated playbook is
    still pending per the Master Index gap tracker) — but Section 12's
    mock loop includes placeholder behavioral prompts so you can
    simulate a full day.
